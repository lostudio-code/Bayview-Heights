01 — The residence

A bright, quiet top floor with the whole city out front.

Newly remodeled and sunny, with a spacious open plan, oversized windows on three sides, and five skylights that pour daylight into the kitchen, hall, and bathrooms. The home lives larger than its 1,300 square feet — and the view does the rest.

Take in amazing panoramic views of the city, the Bay Bridge, and the East Bay hills from this contemporary top-floor home.

Open the front door and the city opens with it — downtown towers anchor the horizon, Bernal Heights rolls off to the west, and the Bay Bridge blinks awake at dusk. The living and dining rooms share one continuous wall of glass; the galley kitchen sits behind, finished with granite counters, stainless appliances, and a gas range under a pyramid skylight.

Down a sun-flooded hallway, three bedrooms tuck away from the street — including a spacious primary with a bay window. Out back, a private deck steps down to a lush, fully fenced yard built for long Sunday barbecues. One block to the Muni T-line, quick access to 101 and 280, and street parking that actually exists.

04 — Neighborhood

Bayview Heights — the next chapter of San Francisco.

Sitting between Bernal Heights and Candlestick Point, Bayview Heights is one of the last hilltop pockets of San Francisco where the view doesn't come with a four-million-dollar price tag. With the Candlestick redevelopment breaking ground and the city's AI-driven recovery in full swing, the neighborhood is in motion.

From your front door to downtown in fifteen minutes.

One block to Muni T-Third, quick onto US-101 and I-280, ferries from Hunters Point — and a hilltop you'll never tire of looking at.

Development · underway

The Candlestick Point redevelopment

A 700-acre transformation of the former stadium site into more than 12,000 homes, retail, parks, and waterfront — one of the largest urban infill projects in the country, and it sits right next door.
