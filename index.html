The residence

A bright, quiet top floor home

Wake up to the city, the Bay Bridge, and the East Bay hills, all from your own top floor.

Open the front door and the whole city is right there. Downtown sits on the horizon, Bernal Heights rolls off to the west, and the Bay Bridge lights up at dusk. The living and dining rooms share one long wall of glass, and the galley kitchen sits just behind, with granite counters, stainless appliances, and a gas range under a pyramid skylight.

Down a sunny hallway you'll find three bedrooms tucked away from the street, including a roomy primary with a bay window. Out back, a private deck steps down to a green, fully fenced yard that's made for long Sunday barbecues. You're a block from the Muni T-line, minutes from 101 and 280, and street parking is easy to find.

Neighborhood

Bayview Heights, the next chapter of San Francisco

Tucked between Bernal Heights and Candlestick Point, Bayview Heights is one of the last hilltop pockets of San Francisco where a view like this is still within reach. With the Candlestick redevelopment breaking ground and the city's AI recovery in full swing, there's a lot happening here. Bayview Hill Park and Ralph D. House Community Park are both a short walk away.

From your front door to downtown in fifteen minutes

One block to Muni T-Third, under a mile to Caltrain, a quick hop onto US-101 and I-280, ferries from Hunters Point, and a hilltop you'll never tire of.
